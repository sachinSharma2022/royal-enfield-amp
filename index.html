REMOVABLE PASSENGER SEAT AND SUBFRAME

The Classic 650 is able to adapt to any ride, any plan or lack thereof, thanks to its flexible design, which offers the option of a removable passenger seat and subframe.

Retro analog instrument cluster

Enjoy the retro style of the speedometer with the modern simplicity of a digital LCD display that lets you track gear position, distance traveled, fuel level and more.

Casquette LED headlight and Tiger headlights

The updated Casquette LED headlight is combined with the iconic Classic "tiger eyes" side lights.

Showa suspension

Wherever you go, proven Classic 650 front suspension and Showa rear suspension will give you confidence in every move.

Chrome dual exhaust

The Classic enters its 650 era with dual Peashooter high-performance exhaust pipes in high-gloss chrome that give it an updated look and a whole new sound.
